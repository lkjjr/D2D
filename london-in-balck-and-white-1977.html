London in Balck and White - 1977

lk October 3, 2026

This Gallery focuses on images captured on 35mm Black & White film while on a trip to London visiting my college roommate Tom Cogswell in 1977. The camera used was a Minolta SRT101, and the films were Kodak PlusX and TriX

 

All the film was developed in my darkroom and 5x7 prints were made there as well.  These images are from recent scans (@300dpi) of a selection of those prints.  They were edited in GIMP open source software to address and spotting or fading over the years.
