35mm Galleries

Gallery of images originally taken on 35mm film. These images come from digitized color slides and scanned copies of photographs.  They have been restored/edited  with open source applications to approach original appearance as closely as possible

Currently experimenting with “best practices” for optimizing images for the web. Main concern is keeping the visual quality as high as possible while also keeping reasonable times for page loading. Could use any feedback on your experience(s) with this.
